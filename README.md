### Restaurant Sales Analysis — Storytelling

**The Story**
I used SQL to explore restaurant order data and understand what customers are actually buying. By connecting menu and order details, I analyzed item popularity, spending behavior, and the composition of the highest-value orders.

**Key Findings**

* Identified the **most and least frequently ordered menu items** and their categories.
* Analyzed the **top 5 highest-spending orders** to understand high-value customer behavior.
* Examined the categories and items included in the **highest-value orders**.
* Found that high-value orders can contain a diverse mix of menu categories, providing opportunities to increase revenue through **cross-selling and menu combinations**.

**Business Impact**
The analysis helps the restaurant understand which products drive demand and what high-spending customers tend to order, supporting better **menu planning, promotions, and upselling strategies**.
